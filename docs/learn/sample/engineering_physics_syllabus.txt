# Engineering Physics — SAMPLE DATA (Syllabus)

Course: PHY 101 — Principles of Classical Mechanics and Oscillations
Scope: Introductory Undergraduate Engineering Physics

1. Kinematics and Reference Frames
   - Position, velocity, and instantaneous acceleration in one and two dimensions
   - Coordinate transformations and inertial reference frames

2. Dynamics and Newton's Laws of Motion
   - Newton's First Law: Law of Inertia
   - Newton's Second Law: Fundamental equation F = m*a
   - Newton's Third Law: Action-reaction pairs and contact forces
   - Free body diagrams and frictional forces

3. Work, Energy, and Power
   - Definition of scalar work done by constant and variable forces
   - The Work-Energy Theorem: W_net = delta(KE)
   - Conservative vs. non-conservative forces and potential energy
   - Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy

4. Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)
   - Hooke's Law and the ideal linear restoring force F = -k*x
   - Differential equation of simple harmonic motion: d^2x/dt^2 + omega^2 * x = 0
   - Period, frequency, and amplitude of a simple pendulum and mass-spring system
   - Energy exchange between kinetic and elastic potential energy in SHM
